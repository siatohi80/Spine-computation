Lumbar Spine / Animation

A rigged L1 to S1 lumbar spine with discs, facet cartilage and ligaments. The spine flexes, extends, bends to the side, twists and lifts 10 kg. Each clear annulus shows the gel nucleus inside it, tinted by the disc load from blue (0 N) to red (3000 N), with a live readout of compression and nucleus pressure at every level. Rendered in Blender Cycles; loads from a Python model driven by posture and body weight (75 kg).
